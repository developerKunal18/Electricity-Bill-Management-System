1. Features
- Register electricity customers
- Generate unique customer IDs
- Record monthly meter readings
- Calculate electricity consumption
- Calculate bills using slab-based tariffs
- Add fixed charges and late fees
- Track paid and unpaid bills
- Record payments and generate receipts
- View customer billing history
- Generate monthly collection reports
